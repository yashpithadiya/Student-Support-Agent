# Student Support Handbook — DEMONSTRATION DATA ONLY

> **DISCLAIMER:** This handbook is entirely fictional and was written for an academic
> assignment. It is NOT an official regulation of any college. Dates, amounts and rules
> are invented for demonstration.

## Fee Payment and Due Dates
Tuition fees are paid in instalments. Instalment due dates for the trimester are published
on the student fee ledger. Fees must be paid through the official online payment portal,
by bank transfer, or by demand draft at the accounts office. Payment is considered
received only when the accounts office confirms it in the fee ledger, which normally
takes one working day after the transaction.

## Late Fee Rules
A late fee of INR 500 per day applies after the due date, capped at INR 10,000 per
instalment. Students with an overdue balance of more than 30 days may be blocked from
registering for examinations until the dues are cleared. Late fees are charged
automatically by the accounts system.

## Payment Portal Failure
If the online payment portal fails or the transaction is declined, the student must keep a
screenshot or transaction reference of the failed attempt. A student who reports the
portal failure to the accounts office within 24 hours may pay on the next working day
without a late fee. If the failure is not reported within 24 hours, the normal late fee
applies. Failed attempts where money was debited are refunded to the original account
within 7 working days. Report portal failures by raising a support request.

## Fee Instalment Plan
Students may split the trimester fee into up to three instalments. A request to change the
instalment plan must be submitted to the accounts office before the first due date.
Plan changes are not permitted after the first instalment is overdue.

## Scholarship Eligibility
The Merit Scholarship is for students with a cumulative CGPA of 3.5 or above and no
active disciplinary record. The Need-Based Aid is for students whose annual family income
is below INR 6,00,000 and who submit an income certificate. The Sports Excellence Award is
for students who have represented the college at state level or higher. A student may hold
only one scholarship at a time.

## Scholarship Application Status Procedure
Scholarship applications move through these statuses: Submitted, Documents Pending, Under
Review, Approved, Rejected. Students can check the status through the student support
desk. Decisions are normally communicated within 15 working days of a complete
application. A rejected applicant may submit one appeal within 10 working days of the
decision, along with the missing or corrected documents.

## Scholarship Renewal
Scholarships must be renewed each academic year. Renewal requires maintaining the minimum
CGPA stated for the scholarship and submitting the renewal form before 31 July.

## Course Change Procedure
Students may request a change of elective course within the first two weeks of the
trimester. The student submits a course change form signed by the faculty advisor and the
programme coordinator. Changes are subject to seat availability. Core courses cannot be
changed. A change of course (specialization) between AI & DS and BDA within the PGDM programme
is not allowed after the first trimester.

## Support Requests and Escalation
Students can raise a support request for issues that cannot be resolved through the
handbook. Each request receives a ticket number. Normal tickets are answered within 3
working days. Urgent tickets, such as payment failures or examination-related issues, are
answered within 1 working day. If a ticket is not resolved within the stated time, the
student may escalate to the Dean of Student Affairs.

## Attendance Policy
A minimum of 75 percent attendance is required in each course to be eligible for the
end-term examination. Students with medical or approved leave may apply for an attendance
condonation, supported by documents, within 5 working days of returning to class.

## Examination Re-evaluation
A student may apply for re-evaluation of an end-term paper within 7 days of the result
being published by paying a fee of INR 1,000 per paper. The result of a re-evaluation is
final.

## Fee Refund Policy
Tuition fees are refundable only if a student withdraws in writing before the first day of the trimester, in which case 90 percent of the paid tuition is refunded. Withdrawal during the first two weeks earns a 50 percent refund. After two weeks no tuition refund is given. Refunds are processed to the original payment source within 15 working days of approval. Registration and examination fees are non-refundable.

## Fee Receipts and Certificates
A fee receipt is generated automatically once the accounts office confirms a payment and can be downloaded from the student portal. A duplicate receipt or a fee-paid certificate can be requested by raising a support request and is issued within 3 working days. Fee certificates for loan or tax purposes carry the financial year of payment.

## Financial Hardship Support
A student facing sudden financial hardship, such as a medical emergency in the family, may apply to the Dean of Student Affairs for a fee deferment of up to 30 days. The application needs a written explanation and supporting documents. A deferment does not remove the late fee exemption automatically; the Dean decides each case in writing.

## Scholarship Disbursement
An approved scholarship is applied as a tuition waiver against the next fee instalment, not paid as cash. The waiver appears on the fee ledger within 10 working days of approval. If the ledger does not show the waiver after that time, the student should raise a support request with the approval letter.

## Scholarship Discontinuation
A scholarship may be discontinued if the student's CGPA falls below the minimum for that scholarship, if the student is found guilty of an academic integrity violation, or if the information in the application is found to be false. The student is informed in writing and may appeal within 10 working days.

## Grading and CGPA
Courses are graded on a 4.0 scale. CGPA is the credit-weighted average of all completed courses. A grade below 2.0 in a course is a fail and the course must be repeated. The CGPA is recalculated after each trimester result and published on the student portal.

## Examination Rules and Hall Ticket
Hall tickets are released 5 working days before the end-term examination to students who meet the attendance requirement and have no fee dues above 30 days. Students must carry the hall ticket and a college identity card. Mobile phones and smart watches are not allowed in the examination hall.

## Backlog and Supplementary Examinations
A student who fails a course may appear in the supplementary examination held after the next trimester. A maximum of two attempts is allowed per course. The supplementary examination fee is INR 2,000 per course. The highest grade awarded in a supplementary attempt is 3.0.

## Leave of Absence
A student may apply for a leave of absence of up to one trimester for medical, family or personal reasons. The application must be approved by the programme coordinator before the leave begins. Fees paid for the trimester are carried forward to the return trimester. A leave of absence longer than one trimester needs the approval of the Dean of Student Affairs.

## Academic Integrity
Plagiarism, copying in examinations, impersonation and use of unauthorised AI-generated work in graded assignments are academic integrity violations. A first violation results in zero marks for the assessment and a written warning. A second violation may lead to suspension for one trimester. Students may present their case before the Academic Integrity Committee.

## Correction of Personal Details
Corrections to a student's name, date of birth, contact details or programme records must be requested through a support request with supporting documents. Changes to the name or date of birth need approval from the registrar's office and take up to 7 working days. A student cannot change these details directly.

## Student Identity Card
The student identity card is issued in the first week of the programme. A lost card can be replaced by raising a support request and paying a fee of INR 300. The identity card is required for the library, examinations and campus entry.

## Grievance Redressal
A student with a complaint about a faculty member, a staff member or a college service should first raise a support request. If the issue is not resolved within 7 working days, the student can submit a written grievance to the Grievance Redressal Committee, which responds within 15 working days. Complaints are handled confidentially.

## Data Privacy and Student Records
Student records are confidential. A student can view only their own fee, scholarship and academic records. Requests to view or change another student's information are refused. Students should never share passwords or one-time passwords with anyone, including staff or the support assistant.
